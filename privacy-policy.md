# Keep In Touch — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 2026-10-07 · Last updated: 2026-10-07*

Keep In Touch ("the app") is made by Greg Nazario ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short
version: **your data stays on your device. We never see it, and there is no
server that could.**

## What the app stores — and where

The app is a personal relationship manager. Everything you create is stored
**only on your device** (the app's private storage on Android; the app's
container on iOS):

- **People you add** — names, keep-up cadence, tier, notes, and your logged
  history of interactions with them.
- **Your settings** — theme preference.

This data never leaves your device. The app contains no networking of its
own: there is no app account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no server
operated by us.

## Contacts

The app never reads your address book. When you use "Import from contacts,"
your device's own contact picker hands the app the **name of the single
contact you chose** — and nothing else. No phone numbers, emails, or other
contact details are accessed, and no contact information is ever uploaded.

## Purchases

If you buy Keep In Touch Pro (subscription or one-time), the purchase is
processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never
receive your payment details. The store tells the app whether an
entitlement is active so the app can unlock Pro features. Refunds,
cancellations, and subscription management happen through your Apple/Google
account, not through us.

## What we don't do

- No analytics or telemetry — the app doesn't measure what you do.
- No tracking, profiling, or advertising — no ad SDKs are included.
- No data collection at all — we could not collect your data if we wanted
  to; there is no backend.
- No third-party data sharing — there is nothing to share.

## Notifications

Nudges ("time to reach out to…") are scheduled **locally on your device**.
They are not push notifications and involve no servers. You can turn the
app's notifications off in your system settings at any time.

## Children

The app is a general-audience personal organizer; it is not directed to
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information
from anyone — of any age.

## Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, you are in full control:

- Deleting a person in the app deletes that person and their history.
- Uninstalling the app deletes **all** app data immediately and permanently.
  There are no server-side copies to request or wait for.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding
optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated here before that change
ships, and the app will ask for your clear consent before any data leaves
your device.
